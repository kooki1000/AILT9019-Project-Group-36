BEng in Computer Science: sample 8-semester route

A sample requirement document for the course planner, built from the HKU BEng (Computer Science)
2025-26 syllabus and its mock study plan, which assumes the English and mathematics exemptions.
Course codes, titles, credits, prerequisites and assessment lines are the syllabus's own, and
prerequisite alternatives that are not in this route are left out. The syllabus has no assessment
details for some courses, so those have none here. The two digital literacy courses are 3 credits
each (6 together), and the first one's title is not in the syllabus. The final year project is
12 credits across Year 4, which the page's data script spreads over both semesters. The Common
Core, the Chinese language course, the courses taken from any department and the summer
internship have no course code here, so the script adds them. The route is not an official
study plan.
