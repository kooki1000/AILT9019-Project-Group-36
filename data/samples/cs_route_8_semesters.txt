BEng in Computer Science: sample 8-semester route

A sample requirement document for the course planner, modelled on the HKU BEng (Computer
Science) 2025-26 syllabus and its mock study plan, which assumes CAES1001 and MATH1011
exemptions. Codes, titles, credits and prerequisites come from the syllabus, and prerequisite
alternatives that are not in this route are left out. The assessment counts are made up for
the demo, because the syllabus gives percentages instead of counts. The Common Core courses,
Chinese language course, free electives and the COMP3410 summer internship have no course code
here, so the page's data script adds them. The syllabus lists no prerequisite for COMP4801, so
the three advanced core courses are added as one to keep it in the final year. The route is not
an official study plan.
