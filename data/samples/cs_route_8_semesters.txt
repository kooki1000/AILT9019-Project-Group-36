BEng in Computer Science: sample 8-semester route

A sample requirement document for the course planner. Course codes, titles, credits and
prerequisites come from the HKU BEng (Computer Science) 2025-26 syllabus; prerequisite
alternatives that are not in this route are left out. The assessment counts are made up
for the demo, because the syllabus gives percentages instead of counts. The route itself
is not an official study plan.
